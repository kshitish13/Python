A dimension table stores descriptive information about an entity. It provides the meaning and context for data.
Examples:
- Customer: customer name, city, age group
- Product: product name, category, brand
- Date: day, month, year, weekday
- Store: store name, location, region
Dimension tables usually have a primary key, such as customer_id or product_id.
A fact table stores events, transactions, or measurements. It contains numbers that can be calculated, summed, averaged, or counted.
Examples:
- Sales transaction: quantity sold, sales amount, discount
- Attendance record: hours present
- Booking: number of guests, room charges
- Website visit: page views, session duration
A fact table usually contains foreign keys that connect it to dimension tables.
